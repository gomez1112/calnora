# Calnora Asset Plan

Images 2.0 was not available as a repo-bound export tool in this Codex environment. The app includes local placeholder PNGs, exact prompts, and an Icon Composer-ready layer manifest. Replace placeholder files with final generated assets before release.

## Shared Safety Rules

- No Apple logos, Apple Health icons, Apple Wallet cards, Apple UI screenshots, or copied Apple artwork.
- No medical diagnosis, clinical treatment cues, before/after bodies, shame-based imagery, celebrity likenesses, or food brand packaging.
- Nutrition visuals should feel approximate, friendly, informational, premium, and non-clinical.
